Privacy first

Your net worth isn't
anyone else's business.

Vundii runs entirely on your Mac. No account creation. No subscriptions. No servers receiving your financial data.

💾

Single SQLite file

All your data lives in one file you choose. Move it to iCloud, Dropbox, or a USB drive — you're in full control. Backup and restore in one click.

Monte Carlo Engine

6,000 futures.
Run in seconds.

Vundii's simulation engine models thousands of random market sequences to give you an honest probability distribution — not a single rosy projection.

🎲

Stochastic paths

Each path draws a unique sequence of annual returns sampled from a distribution calibrated to your equity/bond allocation and inflation rate. No two runs are the same.

6,000
simulation paths per run

Guardrail strategy

Non-discretionary spending is always funded. Discretionary spending cuts 20% if the portfolio falls below 75% of its retirement-date value — automatically, in every path.

guardrail trigger threshold

AI Assistant · Ollama llama3.2 · on-device
You
What happens to my success rate if I retire 2 years earlier?
Vundii AI
Retiring at 58 instead of 60 drops your success rate from 87% to ~74%. Two fewer accumulation years means $24K less in contributions and two more withdrawal years against a smaller portfolio. Run a Scenario to see the full fan chart — the guardrail strategy activates more frequently in the early years.
You
How much Roth conversion room do I have this year?
Vundii AI
$11,200 of headroom in the 22% bracket. Converting that amount costs $2,464 in additional tax but moves funds into tax-free growth. The Tax Planning page shows the full bracket breakdown and year-by-year conversion schedule.
Installation

Download.
Drag. Done.

No Python, no Node.js, no terminal. A self-contained Mac app — unzip and run.

1

Download & install

Unzip the download and drag Vundii.app to your Applications folder.

Vundii-arm64.zip · Apple Silicon
2

Open for the first time

macOS may show an "unidentified developer" warning on first launch. Right-click the app and choose Open, then confirm. You'll never see this again.

3

Activate your license

Paste the license key from your welcome email and enter your email address. Your data stays on your Mac — nothing leaves your machine.
